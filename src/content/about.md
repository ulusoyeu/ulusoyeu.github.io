I am a Mathematics PhD student at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Mauricio Barahona and [Dr. Tolga Birdal](https://tolgabirdal.com). My interest is in the intersection of graph theory, topology, and machine learning. Lately I have been focused around spectral methods on relational systems and their uses in deep learning, a wide area that touches concepts from spectral graph theory, algebraic topology, random walks, signal processing, dynamics and deep learning.

As a broader research goal, I am interested in quantifying the use cases for higher-order relational modeling tools (e.g. simplicial complexes, hypergraphs, cell complexes). Any system we observe exhibits relations between its units, and accurately characterizing when and how we need to use which tools is crucial to make the most of these relations! Always looking for collaborations with people interested in similar questions, or applications of these ideas.

Before this, I studied my MSc in Mathematics at University of Oxford and my BSc in Discrete Mathematics at University of Warwick. Outside research, I am sometimes involved in mathematics outreach, especially with [London Mathematics Outreach](https://www.londonmathsoutreach.org). I am also a big fan of music both as a consumer and creator, I play the guitar and especially enjoy playing funk, indie rock and post-punk. I occasionally play gigs around London.
